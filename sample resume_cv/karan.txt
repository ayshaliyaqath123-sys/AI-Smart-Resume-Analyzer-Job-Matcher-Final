If you’re excited about backend scalability, performance, and seamless integrations—we’d like to meet you!

Design, develop, and maintain APIs and backend services using .NET Core (.NET 6/8) and C#.

Conduct research and proof-of-concepts (PoCs) related to backend automation and tool integrations.

Integrate with external platforms and APIs such as Jira, ServiceNow, Microsoft Defender, and AWS Security Hub.

Participate in Agile development processes, including sprint planning, estimation, and code reviews.

Collaborate with frontend, DevOps, QA, and product teams to deliver production-grade software.

Contribute to architecture discussions, technical design, and mentoring when required.
